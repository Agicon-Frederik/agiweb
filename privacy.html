Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 20, 2026

I'll keep this simple: your privacy matters. This site only uses analytics if you explicitly allow it, and I'll never track you without your consent.

Analytics (With Your Consent)

If you click "Accept" on the cookie banner, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site. This helps me improve the content and user experience.

What's collected when you consent:

  • Pages you visit and how long you stay
  • How you found the site (search engine, direct link, etc.)
  • Basic device info (browser type, screen size)
  • Approximate location (country level only)

What I don't do:

  • Track you across other websites
  • Collect personal information that identifies you
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers
  • Use any tracking if you decline cookies

If you decline cookies, no analytics scripts are loaded and you browse completely anonymously. You can change your preference anytime via the Cookie Settings link in the footer.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

When You Contact Me

If you send me an email at frederik@agicon.be, I'll have your email address and whatever you chose to share. I use this only to respond to you — nothing else. I won't add you to any list unless you explicitly ask me to.

External Links

This site links to external services that have their own privacy policies:

  • LinkedIn — for professional updates
  • YouTube — for video content
  • Substack — for newsletter subscriptions
  • Stan Store — for bookings and resources

When you click these links, you leave my site and enter theirs. I'd recommend checking their privacy policies — I can't control what they do.

Hosting

This website is hosted on standard web hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may log basic access information (like IP addresses) for security and maintenance purposes. This is standard practice and I don't have access to or use this data for any tracking purposes.

Your Rights

Under GDPR (and just basic decency), you have the right to:

  • Know what data I have about you (probably nothing unless you emailed me)
  • Ask me to delete any emails or correspondence
  • Ask questions about any of this

Changes to This Policy

I'll update this page if anything changes about how the site handles your data. The current version reflects the introduction of optional analytics with a consent mechanism.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out:

frederik@agicon.be
