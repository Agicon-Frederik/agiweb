Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

I'll keep this simple: I don't track you. No cookies, no analytics, no creepy stuff following you around the internet.

What I Don't Collect

This website doesn't use:

  • Cookies (not even the "necessary" ones)
  • Analytics or tracking scripts
  • Fingerprinting or any other tracking technology
  • Third-party advertising

You can browse this site without leaving a trace. As it should be.

When You Contact Me

If you send me an email at frederik@agicon.be, I'll have your email address and whatever you chose to share. I use this only to respond to you — nothing else. I won't add you to any list unless you explicitly ask me to.

External Links

This site links to external services that have their own privacy policies:

  • LinkedIn — for professional updates
  • YouTube — for video content
  • Substack — for newsletter subscriptions
  • Stan Store — for bookings and resources

When you click these links, you leave my site and enter theirs. I'd recommend checking their privacy policies — I can't control what they do.

Hosting

This website is hosted on standard web hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may log basic access information (like IP addresses) for security and maintenance purposes. This is standard practice and I don't have access to or use this data for any tracking purposes.

Your Rights

Under GDPR (and just basic decency), you have the right to:

  • Know what data I have about you (probably nothing unless you emailed me)
  • Ask me to delete any emails or correspondence
  • Ask questions about any of this

Changes to This Policy

If I ever add cookies or tracking (unlikely), I'll update this page and add proper consent mechanisms. For now, enjoy the peace of mind.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out:

frederik@agicon.be
