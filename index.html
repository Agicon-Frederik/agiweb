Projects

When not working for clients I use the same mindset to challenge existing systems that are failing. Most of them are in very early stage. Each project tackles a different domain.

Hiring / Talent Systems

Equitalent

Hiring systems were designed to find talent efficiently. But efficiency comes at a cost: candidates who don't match expected patterns get filtered out, even when they'd excel in the role.

Equitalent rethinks selection processes to recognize talent that doesn't fit the mold. It focuses hard on inclusion, neurodivergent people and people with disabilities.

About

I work with organizations who notice that their systems appear to function correctly, yet produce an increasing number of exceptions in practice.

My background spans business psychology, statistics, and software development — a combination that helps me see both why systems fail people and how to rebuild them. I don't just fix symptoms; I analyze where standardized approaches lose their function and design technology that allows context back in.
